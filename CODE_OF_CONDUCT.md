# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Standards

Positive behavior: welcoming language, respecting differing viewpoints, graceful feedback.
Unacceptable: trolling, harassment, publishing private information without permission.

## Enforcement

Report issues to the maintainers. All complaints will be reviewed promptly and fairly.
